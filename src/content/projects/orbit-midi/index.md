Prototype of an arcade game played with a MIDI controller instead of a gamepad. You are a ship, a small triangle,
moving along an arc around a planet: a knob on the controller moves you, and the pads fire small or big shots at
the shapes coming in. The whole game is made of simple shapes. The browser version also works with the keyboard
(arrows or A/D to move, Space for small shots, Shift+Space or X for big shots), and picks up a MIDI controller in
Chrome or Edge.
