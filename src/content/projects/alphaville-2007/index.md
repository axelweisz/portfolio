Short film shot on 35 mm, co-produced by my company Zøi Filmes with FAAP and Paulinho Caruso; I was the executive
producer. It is an allegory of class relations in Brazil, and its title plays on Alphaville, the famous gated
community on the outskirts of São Paulo. It won Best Short Film at the Gramado Film Festival, Brazil's most
important film festival.
