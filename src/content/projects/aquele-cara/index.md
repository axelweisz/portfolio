Animated short based on a story by the Brazilian cartoonist Laerte Coutinho. It follows a man with an ordinary
face in a world where everyone else has a thumb for a head. Produced after winning a São Paulo State grant,
animated in Flash and After Effects and printed to 35 mm film. I was the executive producer.
