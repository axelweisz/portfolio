Independent documentary ("Operation Trojan Horse") following a group of young ravers trying to crash a party on
the outskirts of São Paulo. I co-directed it with Laura Faerman and Thiago Villas Boas, and produced it. It won
Best Short Documentary at both the Mix Brasil festival and the Cuiabá Film Festival.
