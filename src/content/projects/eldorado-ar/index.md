An augmented reality treasure hunt for Black Friday 2017 at Shopping Eldorado in São Paulo, where the treasure
was the discounts. Visitors scanned images placed around the mall to reveal discount coupons, given out at random,
with a draw for a single 99% discount. Push notifications told people where in the building the biggest
discounts were at certain moments. Built at Halem, where I developed the AR app.
